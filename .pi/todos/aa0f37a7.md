## Problem
`work.html` has 54 `style="..."` attributes. `receive.html` has 17. Other templates
have 1-5 each. This defeats Tailwind's purpose and makes responsive design harder.

## Fix
- Extract repeated patterns into `@layer components` in `input.css`:
  - `.work-detail-layout` (flex, gap, wrap)
  - `.metadata-row` (font-size, color, flex, gap)
  - `.admin-panel` (border, padding, bg, radius)
  - `.inline-edit-form` (flex, gap, align)
- Convert remaining one-off inline styles to Tailwind utilities where appropriate
- Add responsive breakpoints (`@media` or Tailwind `sm:`/`md:`) for:
  - Work detail page (stack cover above text on mobile)
  - Edition cards (stack vertically on narrow screens)
  - Nav links (hamburger or wrap)

## Scope
- `input.css` — new component classes (~30 lines)
- All 20 templates — replace inline styles
- `make css` to rebuild
- Large diff but no behavior change
